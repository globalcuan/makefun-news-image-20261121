This entry is a program that writes one image summing up the world news of Saturday 21 November 2026. `compose.py` draws `entry.png` with Pillow from the day's reported headlines; `main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.

Stories shown:

- Iran strikes US bases in Bahrain, fires at an industrial site, and tanker attacks near Hormuz — reports of 21 November 2026: Al Jazeera.
- Russian missiles land in Poland and a strike kills two in Kryvyi Rih — reports of 21 November 2026: Associated Press.
- AI selloff and market correction — reports of 21 November 2026: BBC.
- Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen — reports of 21 November 2026: France 24.
- Mexico captures cartel boss “Chapo Isidro” — reports of 21 November 2026: Associated Press.
- COP31 climate talks deadlock over fossil fuels — reports of 21 November 2026: One Turn News.
- Myanmar genocide ruling, GTA VI launch, Peru earthquake and James Harden's scoring milestone — reports of 21 November 2026: BBC.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
It writes /output/entry.png (1024x1024 PNG).
